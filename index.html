Ground floor

Trattoria da Ciro

Quartieri Spagnoli, Naples

In the narrow streets above Via Toledo, Da Ciro has fed the neighbourhood for three generations. The wood-fired oven never stops, the pizza is tossed in the air, and old Ciro still shouts the orders across the room. You share a table, the drinks come fast, and the whole place smells of basil and warm dough. Loud, generous Naples — it makes you feel at home the moment you sit down.

Pizza & cocktails in the courtyard · from 7 PM
1st floor

Byeol Noraebang

Hongdae, Seoul

Up some neon stairs in Hongdae, Seoul's lively student district, Byeol is the karaoke bar the locals love. Soft booths, a long list of K-pop hits, a tambourine, and a piano in the corner for anyone brave enough. No one is judging — you grab a mic, the room sings along, and the night flies by. Simply the most fun you can have with a songbook.

Piano bar & karaoke · from 6:30 PM
2nd floor

The Laneway

Melbourne, Australia

Hidden down one of Melbourne's painted laneways, behind a plain metal door, The Laneway is a friendly local pub. Exposed brick, an old world map on the wall, fresh beers on tap, and a back room full of pinball, darts and a worn pool table. Grab a beer, lose a game, win the next one. Relaxed and easygoing — the Australian way of taking life lightly.

Games — by the CSE · from 6:30 PM
6th floor

Puerta 1928

Palermo, Buenos Aires

There's no sign on Puerta 1928 — just a heavy door on a quiet Palermo street. Knock, and another era opens up: brass, green velvet, a long bar and a trio playing soft jazz. There's no cocktail menu — you tell the bartender how you feel, and he makes your drink. A secret kept on purpose, and enjoyed slowly.

Hidden bar, signature cocktails · 7:30–9:30 PM

Basement

Klub Nacht

Berlin

In an old boiler room under the street, Klub Nacht is Berlin at its purest: bare concrete, no VIP, no photos — just the music. Fog floats through the lights, the bass fills your chest, and there are no rules. When the beat drops, the whole room moves together. This is where the night turns into a memory.

Music, dancing, DJs · from 9:30 PM
